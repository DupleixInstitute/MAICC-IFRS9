## 6. EIR and Revenue Recognition Engine

### 6.1 The three doors

IFRS 9 uses the effective interest rate in three places, and the engine serves all three:

| Door | What it does | Where |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Measurement | Constructs amortised cost as a roll-forward: closing = opening + advances + interest + unwind minus cash and derecognition, adjusted for confirmed restructures, replacing the tape-imported carrying amount | `app/Services/Eir/EirRevenueService.php`, `ModificationAccountingService.php` |
| 2 Revenue | Recognises interest as EIR times gross carrying amount for stages 1 and 2, and for stage 3 on the governed basis (`stage3_interest_basis`, by default the net carrying amount, gross less ECL allowance, IFRS 9 paragraph 5.4.1(b)) | `EirRevenueService.php` |
| 3 Impairment | Discounts expected shortfalls at the original EIR, or at the current EIR for a floating-rate loan (B5.5.44) | `app/Services/Ecl/TimePhasedEclService.php`, `EclDiscountRateService.php` (also used by `app/Jobs/CalculateDiscountingJob.php` for LGD recoveries) |

The engine reads the live stage and the ECL allowance from `loan_books` and writes to its own tables (chapter 3.9); the one exception is reopening a locked EIR, which marks the contract's discounted ECL on `loan_books` stale (6.8). There is no fallback discount rate: a loan whose rate cannot be resolved is left undiscounted and named, and the horizon-proxy discounting service that existed until 14 October 2026 has been removed. The authoritative specification is `docs/MAIIC_EIR_Engine_Specification_v4_2026-10-07.md` (the earlier `docs/MAIIC_EIR_Revenue_Recognition_Engine_Spec.md` is kept for history); where the two differ, the code described here is what runs.

### 6.2 Pipeline

```
accounting rules -> intake (Extracts A, B, C, schedules, fees) and the E-Banker feed
-> GL fee route and fee source precedence -> fee classification (maker, checker)
-> tranche register and schedule governance (draft, approved by a second person)
-> readiness gate -> solver -> lock (maker, checker)
-> monthly revenue run (write-offs, restructures, rate re-estimation) -> GL reconciliation
-> coverage and blockers -> period lock
```

The EIR screens are gated in their controller constructors:

| Permission | Screens and actions |
|---|---|
| `settings` | Accounting Rules, Fee Classification (classify, review, return, re-apply the rulebook), schedule generate and approve (`EirScheduleController`), EIR Calculations (calculate, approve, reopen), running and recalculating the revenue from the reconciliation screen |
| `settings` or `eir.fees.import` | EIR Data Intake, where `eir.fees.import` opens the fee type only; reading Fee Classification and its fee GL check |
| `eir.view` | EIR Data, Drawdowns, Reference Rates, Trial Balances, Write-offs, Restructures & Resets, Imports & Feeds (E-Banker loads, builds, periods), Take-on, the coverage tabs, EIR as at a Date, GL Reconciliation |
| `eir.govern` | Governance Centre; landing a pack, proposing and approving a loan book build, locking and unlocking a period (`EirFeedController`); uploading a trial balance; take-on upload, build, confirm and fees; proposing, approving and closing write-offs, restructures and rate resets; building the reset schedules (`eir-restructures.resets.build`); the locked-EIR review check (`eir-calculations.lock-review.detect`); tying a held GL fee line (`tieHeld`); staging thresholds |
| `eir.export` | The reconciliation export and the auditor pack |

The menu hides each tab from a user without its permission (chapter 2.3); the Fee Classification tab is listed under `settings`, so a user with only `eir.fees.import` may open the page but does not see the tab. An administrator may act as the checker on their own work only where the governed setting `maker_checker_admin_override` is "Allowed for administrators" (`GovernanceService::adminOverrideAllowed`); its default is "Not allowed" (audit finding H9).

### 6.3 Fee rulebook

`eir_accounting_rules` hold match conditions (fee type, GL account, cash flow direction, description contains) and a boolean proposed treatment `proposed_integral`. Fee types are arrangement, legal, appraisal, default, levy and other (`FeeImportService::KNOWN_TYPES`). `app/Services/Eir/FeeRuleMatcher.php` considers only active, approved rules, ordered by priority then id, and the first rule whose non-null conditions all match wins; exclusions are seeded above inclusions so a general "legal costs are integral" rule cannot swallow recovery litigation. A bare "legal" or "other" line matches nothing on purpose so a person decides. `sweepPending()` re-applies the approved rulebook to PENDING lines only and reports examined, matched, unmatched, changed and left-alone counts. Rules are seeded unapproved by `EirAccountingRuleSeeder`; editing a rule resets its approval. Rule approval has no maker and checker gate.

### 6.4 Fee classification

`app/Http/Controllers/EirFeeClassificationController.php` runs the maker and checker. Classify (manual mode with an explicit integral flag, or rule mode applying each line's own matched rule) moves PENDING lines to CLASSIFIED with the classifier's id and reason; in rule mode a line without a matched rule is refused rather than defaulted. Review moves CLASSIFIED to REVIEWED and refuses when the reviewer classified the line (unless the governed administrator override applies) or when an integral line has no cash-flow direction. Return to classification (`eir-fee-classification.return`, with a reason) sends CLASSIFIED or REVIEWED lines back to PENDING for a fresh classification and review, and is refused for a contract whose original EIR is locked until that EIR is reopened. Every decision is appended to `eir_fee_classification_events`. Only lines that are REVIEWED, integral and not superseded enter the solver (`EirContractInputService::reviewedIntegralFees`).

The screen also lists the GL fee lines the GL fee route could not place on one loan (`fee_gl_unassigned`, 6.13), with the reason and any candidate loan the evidence points to, and the differences between the fee source of record and a lower source. A person with `eir.govern` ties a held line to its loan with a reason (`eir-fee-classification.tie-held`, `GlFeeBuildService::assignHeld`, audit-logged); the line enters `contract_fees` PENDING and still needs its classification and review.

### 6.5 Schedule governance

Extract B carries remaining cash flows, not the original promise, so the original schedule is imported, taken from the take-on workbook (6.12), or generated from Extract A terms by `app/Services/Eir/ScheduleGeneratorService.php`. The generator builds only shapes E-Banker can express: a level instalment (EMI type E) or equal principal (type P); a moratorium of type "Principle Only" (interest paid, balance flat) or "Both" (interest added monthly, instalment sized on the enlarged balance), run from the first disbursement; any of 1, 2, 4, 6 or 12 payments a year; dated periods under the governed `day_count` and `period_rate_basis`; and, for a facility drawn in tranches, the tranches as the ledger drew them, each earning interest from its own date with the instalment re-sized when a tranche arrives. A partly drawn facility whose contract and scheme state no interest basis reads the basis from E-Banker's postings or the governed `partly_drawn_interest_basis`; anything else is refused with the reason. Scheme settings come from the landed scheme master by `php artisan eir:load-schemes` (SM_01, then DD_10 where SM_01 lacks a field), which also flags contracts drawn above their sanction.

`app/Services/Eir/ScheduleWorkflowService.php` writes generated rows as version 1 with source GENERATED and status DRAFT, recording who prepared them, and never generates over a version 1 held from another source (a take-on schedule is moved to PENDING_REVIEW instead). It compares the draft with E-Banker's remaining schedule from its earliest due date, instalment by instalment up to the point E-Banker regenerated its chart: WITHIN_TOLERANCE when the cash difference on the compared instalments is within one percent (floor MK 1), otherwise CASH_VARIANCE, or NOT_COMPARABLE or NO_REMAINING_DATA. Approval takes a DRAFT or PENDING_REVIEW schedule, needs a review note when the comparison is outside tolerance, and is governed by `schedule_approval_control`: under "Second person must approve" (the default) the approver must be named and must not be the preparer. The bootstrap approves under its own label (`schedule_approver_label`). An approved schedule cannot be regenerated, except one built without the tranche register while the EIR is unlocked, which returns to a draft for a second approval. Restructures write later versions (6.15).

### 6.6 Readiness gate

`app/Services/Eir/EirReadinessService.php` returns READY or BLOCKED with named issues. Blockers: `CONTRACT_PROFILE_MISSING`, `EQUITY_EXCLUDED`, `EIR_LOCKED`, `ORIGINATION_DATE_MISSING`, `DRAWN_AMOUNT_MISSING`, `FREQUENCY_INVALID`, `FREQUENCY_ASSUMED` (frequency not stated by a source), `SCHEDULE_NOT_APPROVED`, `ORIGINAL_SCHEDULE_MISSING`, `TAKEON_NOT_RECOMPUTED` and `TAKEON_SOLVE_REFUSED` (take-on loans, 6.12), `SCHEDULE_INVALID`, `SCHEDULE_DATE_INVALID`, `SCHEDULE_BEFORE_FIRST_DRAWDOWN` (an instalment due on or before the first money advanced), `SCHEDULE_NOT_ON_TRANCHES` (a generated schedule that does not follow the tranche register), `SCHEDULE_DATE_DUPLICATE`, `PRINCIPAL_NOT_RECONCILED` (scheduled principal more than one percent from the expected contractual principal, which for a generated schedule is the balance the generator recorded it amortises), `FEE_CLASSIFICATION_PENDING`, `FEE_DIRECTION_MISSING` and `INITIAL_NET_INVALID`. For a take-on loan the take-on check stands in for the date and principal checks. A fee line superseded by a higher source neither blocks nor enters the EIR. The initial net investment is the amount first drawn (the take-on chain's opening for a take-on loan) minus integral fees received plus integral costs paid.

`app/Services/Eir/EirContractInputService.php` turns a ready contract into the solver input and returns an immutable snapshot: the version 1 schedule in due-date order, reviewed integral fees only, and for a tranched facility the solve plan of `DisbursementService::solvePlan` (the first drawdown as the investment on its own date, each later tranche as a negative flow marked `DISBURSEMENT`). A take-on loan is assembled from its checked take-on chain and the take-on workbook's fees.

### 6.7 Solver

`app/Services/Eir/CalculateEirService.php` is a pure periodic internal rate of return solver with no database access. Newton-Raphson runs up to 100 iterations with a bisection fallback of up to 250 iterations; the result is accepted only if the residual net present value is within one part in a billion of the initial investment. Outputs are always labelled: `eir_period`, `eir_nominal_annual` (period rate times payments per year) and `eir_effective_annual` ((1 + period rate) to the power of payments per year, minus one). The date-sensitive entry point discounts on the actual contractual dates under the contract's day-count basis (ACT/365, ACT/360, 30/360, 30E/360) and solves the effective annual rate directly, so an irregular first period or a moratorium does not distort the rate; `EirCalculationService` chooses it whenever every cash flow carries a due date. Validation refuses a non-positive initial investment, an unsupported frequency, duplicate periods and negative receipts; a negative flow is accepted only when it is marked as a later drawdown (`flow_type` `DISBURSEMENT`).

The ACADES golden case (eight quarterly instalments of MK 17,099,839.71 on net proceeds of MK 95,990,000) must return a quarterly rate near 8.6217 percent, nominal 34.49 percent and effective 39.21 percent (`tests/Unit/Eir/CalculateEirServiceTest.php`).

### 6.8 Calculation, lock and reopening

`app/Services/Eir/EirCalculationService.php` writes the result to `contract_eir` with status CALCULATED, the solver method, iterations and residual, and the input snapshot; a failure sets BLOCKED with the error. `app/Jobs/CalculateEirJob.php` processes a batch and a blocked contract does not stop the batch. Locking requires a CALCULATED result with an identifiable calculator and a reviewer different from the calculator (unless the governed administrator override applies); it sets LOCKED and is final. The bootstrap may lock in place of a second person only under its own label (`LoanBookBuildService::BOOTSTRAP_LABEL`, "System Bootstrap (automated data-readiness, not a MAIIC approval)"), which is stored in `contract_eir.locked_label`; any other label is refused.

Reopening needs the governed administrator override (`EirCalculationController::reopen` returns 403 otherwise) and a reason of at least ten characters, and is refused while any month the contract's roll-forward or discounted ECL reached is locked (6.16). It archives the locked result to `eir_calculation_history`, moves every amortisation row to `eir_amortisation_history`, marks the contract's ECL discounting as `STALE_EIR_REOPENED` and returns the contract to REOPENED for recalculation and fresh approval. Recalculating an unlocked contract archives the previous result with the reason "Recalculated before final approval".

A locked EIR is flagged for review when fee evidence for the loan was not in the inputs it was locked on (`app/Services/Eir/EirLockReviewService.php`). `detect()` raises a flag for each locked contract where a held GL fee posting names the loan as its likely owner, or a fee line of a source in force (not superseded) was recorded after the lock; a flag already raised for the same evidence is left as it is, so the same evidence is never flagged twice. `php artisan eir:lock-review --detect` runs it, and `--raise= --reason= --evidence=` flags a loan by hand where the evidence is outside the system. The detect is also on the screen: EIR Calculations, **Check locked EIRs** (`eir-calculations.lock-review.detect`, `EirCalculationController::detectReviews`, needing `eir.govern`). A flag never reopens an EIR or changes a figure; a second person clears it with a reason (`eir-calculations.lock-review.clear`; the person who raised it cannot), or it closes when a reopening of the EIR is approved.

### 6.9 Revenue run

`php artisan eir:run-revenue {period} --user=` (queued with `--queue`; `--recalculate --reason=` to restate) runs `app/Jobs/RunEirRevenueJob.php` over locked contracts. A locked period is refused. Per contract and month, `EirRevenueService` computes:

```
eirInForce  = FIXED ? locked eir_effective_annual
            : (1 + max(0, nominal(locked EIR) + loanBookRate - builtRate) / 12)^12 - 1
monthlyRate = (1 + eirInForce)^(1/12) - 1
basis       = stage 3 ? stage3_interest_basis (default NET) : GROSS
interest    = monthlyRate x (basis NET ? max(0, opening - allowance) : opening)
unwind      = basis NET ? monthlyRate x min(allowance, opening) : 0
advances    = basis LEDGER ? each tranche drawn in the month, net of the month's integral fees : 0
interest   += monthlyRate x each advance x (days outstanding in the month / days in the month)
closing     = max(0, opening + advances + interest + unwind - cash - derecognised)
closing     = the closing after any confirmed restructure of the month (6.15)
```

`builtRate` is the rate the locked schedule was built at: the contract master's rate, or for a take-on loan the no-fee rate of its own schedule. After a confirmed restructure the locked EIR and built rate are those the restructure set (`ModificationAccountingService::basisBefore`).

Opening is the prior month's closing. The first month opens on one of two bases, recorded on every row as `opening_basis` and carried forward with the closing balance:

- **SCHEDULE**: the present value, at the locked rate, of the contractual flows due after the end of the month before. (Until 13 October 2026 the value was taken at the end of the month itself, which counted that month's interest twice.) Where the ledger at that date owes more than the balance the schedule expects (the same flows at `contractual_rate`) by more than one percent, the difference is booked as arrears and added, undiscounted; the first row holds it as `opening_arrears` and the audit log as `EIR Opening Arrears` (decision D39).
- **LEDGER**: the drawn balance in the E-Banker ledger at the end of the month before, used when the facility is still drawing down (an advance falls after that date), when it was lent within the month (the opening is then nil), or when the borrower has prepaid (the ledger is below half the present value). Each later tranche is added in the month it is drawn and earns interest from that day; the row's `advances` column holds it. A schedule-basis row never adds an advance, because the schedule's present value already counts every tranche. Each ledger opening is written to the audit log as `EIR Opening From Ledger` with both figures and the reason.

A loan migrated at the take-on of 31 July 2024 starts its roll-forward in August 2024; a run for July 2024 or earlier is refused for it. A floating-rate loan (`contract_eir.rate_type` FLOATING) accrues at the EIR in force for the month: the locked nominal EIR moved by the change from the built rate to the rate E-Banker charged that month (`loan_books.interest_rate`), keeping the margin the fees add (IFRS 9 B5.4.5, decision D38). Every row records the effective annual rate it accrued at as `eir_annual`; the GL reconciliation, `EirAsAtService` and the ECL discount rate (`currentFloatingEir`) read it. The stage (`calculated_ifrs9_stage`, then the post-qualitative stage) is read from the loan book row for the month and the allowance from the prior month's `ecl_value`.

Cash comes from `eir_actual_transactions` whenever the feed covers the month (the feed's postings run past the month's start and the contract has a transaction by its end; audit finding C5); a covered month with no receipt is nil cash. `php artisan eir:load-ledger-cash` copies the E-Banker ledger into that table under source `EBANKER_LEDGER`, pairing each reversal with the posting it reverses. Only Principal+Interest and Fee rows count as cash; Write-off and Settlement in kind rows are derecognition, which reduces the gross without being cash and is shown on the row as a negative `modification_gain_loss`; Disbursement rows are advances; any other type is reported as unclassified. A month the feed does not cover takes the schedule version in force and is marked DERIVED. Rerunning a period archives that period and every later one for the contract, because each opening is the prior closing; recalculation without a reason is refused.

The recalculation is also on the screen: GL Reconciliation, **Recalculate (month)...** on the amortised-cost roll-forward card (`eir-reconciliation.recalculate-revenue`, `EirReconciliationController::recalculateRevenue`, needing `settings`; shown only when the month holds `eir_amortisation` rows). It takes a reason of at least 20 characters and optional `contract_ids` (every named contract must have a locked EIR), runs `RunEirRevenueJob` for the month with `--recalculate` semantics and the reason, then runs every later period that held rows of those contracts again, in order, in the same request, so the chain is never left with a hole. It is refused before anything is written when the month holds no rows, when a named contract has no locked EIR, or when the month or any later calculated month is locked. The console command does not make that last check itself: `EirRevenueService::supersede` removes the later rows of the contract even where a later month is locked, and leaves the later periods for the operator to re-run. Prefer the screen.

### 6.10 GL reconciliation

`app/Services/Eir/EirGlReconciliationService.php` compares, per contract and month, the interest the ledger posted (`gl_interest_postings`), the contractual interest the contract should have posted (`ContractualInterestService`: the prior month-end balance, the rate in force and the actual days under the governed day count) and the interest the engine accrued, and resolves the variance into terms that sum to it:

```
base effect            = contractual interest - GL posted
carrying amount effect = contractual convention on the engine's opening - contractual interest
rate effect            = monthly EIR x opening - contractual convention on the engine's opening
impairment effect      = accrued - monthly EIR x opening
unexplained            = variance - the four effects
```

The monthly EIR is the row's `eir_annual` (else the locked rate). Rows inside the governed band (`recon_tolerance` in force at the period end, a percentage with an absolute floor) are WITHIN_TOLERANCE; every other difference carries a named cause: NO_POSTING, LATE_DISBURSEMENT, CATCH_UP_POSTING, MID_MONTH_TRANCHE, RATE_MISMATCH, DATA_GAP or, as a last resort, UNEXPLAINED. Postings with no calculated counterpart are NOT_CALCULATED or NO_CONTRACT and stay out of the bridge as coverage gaps. The screen exports to Excel or PDF (`eir-reconciliation.export`, needing `eir.export`).

`app/Services/Eir/TrialBalanceMovementService.php` supplies control totals from the trial balances: movement equals this month's balance minus last month's, January taken whole, balance sheet accounts never differenced, a missing prior month refused, the pre-closing basis preferred for December. Scope comes from `gl_account_scope` so investment income codes and QuickBooks duplicates are excluded. Finance uploads the month's E-Banker trial balance on EIR Data, Trial Balances (`eir-trial-balances.upload`, needing `eir.govern`) through `TrialBalanceLandingService`'s gates: a known month, debits equal to credits, and the report's grand total.

### 6.11 Coverage and blockers

`app/Services/Eir/EirCoverageService.php` profiles the whole book in bulk queries and mirrors the core readiness rules (a test asserts the two agree contract by contract on the shared fixtures). It reports contracts in scope, covered (locked), coverage percent by count and exposure, blockers ranked by exposure with sole-blocker counts, and coverage by portfolio. The Mega Farm programme (decision D30) and equity instruments are counted apart as out of scope. Known divergences from `EirReadinessService`: coverage tests scheduled principal against the drawn amount rather than the balance the generator recorded, and it does not raise the take-on codes (`TAKEON_NOT_RECOMPUTED`, `TAKEON_SOLVE_REFUSED`) or the tranche codes (`SCHEDULE_BEFORE_FIRST_DRAWDOWN`, `SCHEDULE_NOT_ON_TRANCHES`), and it applies the date and principal checks to take-on schedules.

### 6.12 Tranches and take-on EIRs

The tranche register (`contract_disbursements`, read by `app/Services/Eir/DisbursementService.php`) is built from the ledger; a fee debited to the loan in the drawdown batch is added to its drawdown rather than made a tranche of its own, and is held in `capitalised_fees`, which the Drawdowns screen shows per tranche and per facility. Schedules follow each drawdown (6.5) and the solve starts from the first money advanced (6.6).

A loan migrated at the take-on of 31 July 2024 takes its version 1 schedule from Finance's take-on workbook (schedule source `TAKEON_WORKBOOK`), landed by `app/Services/Ebanker/TakeonLandingService.php` (`php artisan eir:land-takeon`, or Imports & Feeds, Take-on). The build of `contract_takeon` is proposed by one person and approved by a different person (`takeon_build_requests`; `eir-takeon.build` proposes when nothing is pending and approves otherwise; `--build` and `--approve-build=` on the console); only the approval builds, and a later workbook landed since the proposal refuses it. Under the governed `takeon_history_basis` each loan is recomputed from origination or left on its take-on balance. Every recomputed solve is checked under `takeon_eir_plausibility`: the schedule must retire the loan, and its no-fee rate must lie inside the lowest and highest rate its own lines charge, widened by the governed margin (0.5 point by default). The result is stored as `contract_takeon.eir_check` (PASS or REFUSED) with the reason, and shown on the Take-on screen. A passing schedule is moved to PENDING_REVIEW, approved on Schedule Review like any draft, calculated and locked through the normal lock; take-on fees land in `contract_fees` as PENDING lines and never compete under the fee source precedence.

### 6.13 GL fee route and fee source precedence

`php artisan eir:build-gl-fees` (`app/Services/Ebanker/GlFeeBuildService.php`, a step of the month-end) ties the fee GL postings (GL_04a, with every leg of the same batches in GL_04b) to their loans: one loan in the batch; else the loan whose own debit equals the fee, then the loan the narration names; else the loan GL_07 shows drawn through GL 2010 by the same amount. Anything else, and every Mega Farm programme fee, is held in `fee_gl_unassigned` with the reason, never guessed (6.4). Tied lines land in `contract_fees` PENDING with source "E-Banker GL fee posting".

`app/Services/Eir/FeeSourcePrecedence.php` then applies the governed `fee_source_precedence` (default: GL posting, Finance workbook, LOS capture, Extract A): for each contract the highest source holding any fee wins, and every line from a lower source is marked `superseded_by_source`, kept and shown but never entering the EIR or the fee GL check. The build also reports the fee GL gate: each month's GL_04a movement on each fee GL (4871, 4873, 4874, 4875) against the trial balance, AGREES, DIFFERENCE or NO_TB. The same gate runs at the pack door (`PackGates`), where a difference of a kwacha or more fails the pack.

### 6.14 Write-offs

`app/Services/Ebanker/WriteOffClassifier.php` decides which credits to a loan account are write-offs rather than collections: transaction type 300; a credit in a batch whose other leg debits GL 1064 (read from GL_05, GL_03 and M09_01); or an "ACCOUNT CLOSURE" credit in a TRF batch on an account with status H. The loan book build, the contract inputs, the ledger cash and the reports all ask this one class.

Write-offs not on the loan account go through the governed register `loan_write_offs` (`app/Services/Eir/WriteOffRegisterService.php`, EIR Data, Write-offs, routes `eir-write-offs.index`, `.store`, `.approve`, `.close`). One person proposes with the reason and evidence; the period lock is checked at the proposal. A different person approves (no administrator override), the lock is checked again, and the write-off is written to `eir_actual_transactions` as a Write-off with source `WRITE_OFF_REGISTER`, which the revenue run treats as derecognition. A proposal can be rejected by a reviewer or withdrawn by its proposer; an approved entry can be reversed with a reason while its month is open, which removes its transaction. A contract can have only one write-off proposed or approved at a time.

### 6.15 Restructures, modification accounting and rate resets

`app/Services/Eir/RestructureCandidateService.php` finds candidate restructures in E-Banker's reschedule history: regenerations of the instalment chart (P2_10 and the superseded rows of RS_02a) that extend the maturity, capitalise arrears or fall on a date with no rate change; differences between the dated chart copies (RS_02b to RS_02d, which fail closed for want of a date); accepted term edits in MODIFICATION_LOG (RS_02f); and the restructures on the take-on migration worksheet (CV_10). `php artisan eir:restructure-candidates` (`--only=candidates` or `--only=resets`) runs detection only; the bootstrap and the month-end run it, and nothing is ever confirmed by it.

`app/Services/Eir/RestructureRegisterService.php` holds `restructure_events` under maker-checker (Restructures & Resets; `EirRestructureController`, routes `eir-restructures.index`, `.detect`, `.propose`, `.approve`, `.close`). One person proposes a classification (MODIFICATION, REVISED_ESTIMATE, CONTRACTUAL_RESET, CORRECTION or NOT_A_CHANGE) with a reason, any qualitative factors, the Board approval reference, and a fair value with its basis where needed; a different person approves (the governed administrator override applies). A classification that changes the accounting needs a date, the old terms and E-Banker's new schedule, and an open month. On approval the new chart is loaded as schedule version N+1 (`contract_cashflow_schedule.effective_from` the event date) and a PENDING row is opened in `contract_modifications`. A proposal can be rejected or withdrawn, and a confirmed event reversed with a reason while its month is open and no later restructure of the loan stands. An event on or before the take-on date is history and disclosure only.

`app/Services/Eir/ModificationAccountingService.php` applies each confirmed event in the revenue run of its month, on the month's closing:

- **MODIFICATION** (IFRS 9 5.4.3): the present value of version N+1 at the EIR the month accrued at is tested against the carrying amount before under the governed `modification_threshold` (10 percent by default; B3.3.6 by analogy). Below it, with no qualitative factor, the gross becomes that present value and the difference is the modification gain or loss; modification fees adjust the carrying amount and set a revised EIR. At or above it, or with a qualitative factor, the loan is derecognised (3.2.3) and the new asset recognised at the recorded fair value with a new EIR; the difference is the derecognition gain or loss.
- **REVISED_ESTIMATE**, and **CONTRACTUAL_RESET** on a fixed-rate loan: the B5.4.6 catch-up to the present value of the revised flows at the original EIR. A contractual reset on a floating-rate loan books nothing further: the monthly re-estimation (6.9) already carries it.

Anything that cannot be applied (no flows after the month-end, no fair value recorded on a derecognition, a derecognised stage 3 loan that would be purchased or originated credit-impaired, no governed threshold) throws `ModificationBlockedException`; the month is refused for the contract and the register row is marked BLOCKED with the reason. A later month is refused until every confirmed event before it is booked. The row records `modification_adjustment`, `adjustment_kind` and `schedule_version` on `eir_amortisation`.

Rate resets are detected once. `app/Services/Eir/ResetDetectionService.php` is the one detector of prime-rate resets: for each loan whose contract master rate type is FLOATING (the same test D38 applies), every change of the PLR series since drawdown on the spread locked at drawdown (`margin_basis`), dated under `plr_mid_period` (by default the 1st of the month the change falls in, the whole month at the new rate, section 3.1), written as a PLR_RESET row of `rate_reset_events` with the loan's `eir_rate_periods`; a loan with no rate type or an unknown timing rule is held. `app/Services/Eir/RateResetService.php` runs it first and then sets each reset against the loan book's month-end rate: the pipeline's row carries the rates charged and the spreads as evidence; a spread that moved is a SPREAD_CHANGE row of its own, even on the date of a PLR reset; a move with the PLR that the pipeline has no reset for (the loan is held there, or the engine treats it as fixed-rate) is recorded as a PLR_RESET held from the pipeline (status BLOCKED, key MOVE|) with the reason; a fixed-rate loan's other moves go to the restructure register as candidates. `php artisan eir:restructure-candidates` (bootstrap and month-end) and `php artisan eir:detect-resets` both run this one detection. The register's maker-checker state is `classification_status`: one person proposes IN_CONTRACT or NEGOTIATED with a reason, a different person approves, and a move in a locked period is refused; a NEGOTIATED move becomes a restructure candidate. The governed `rate_change_classification` can close NEGOTIATED altogether. `php artisan eir:build-reset-schedules --user=` (`ResetScheduleService`, not run by the bootstrap or the month-end; also on the screen as Restructures & Resets, Rate resets, **Build reset schedules**, `eir-restructures.resets.build`, needing `eir.govern`, which reports the versions built, kept and held with each held loan's reason, for example NOT_FLOATING or NO_VERSION_1, and is safe to re-run) re-amortises the remaining instalments at each PLR reset of a floating loan as a RESET schedule version and sets the pipeline's `status` to SCHEDULED; it skips spread changes, held moves and resets approved as negotiated, and holds a reset after a confirmed restructure. A Reset version is the re-estimated contractual flows only (it is what EIR as at a date shows as the remaining flows); neither it nor `eir_rate_periods` sets an EIR, so the rate change is applied once, by D38.

The ECL of a floating-rate loan is discounted at its current EIR, the rate the month accrued at (`EirRevenueService::currentFloatingEir`, IFRS 9 B5.5.44); where that cannot be determined the loan is left undiscounted and named, never discounted at the original EIR in its place.

### 6.16 Period lock

A locked month is never restated (`reporting_period_locks`, spec decision D21). `php artisan eir:lock-period {YYYY-MM} --user= --reason=` locks a month and snapshots every governance value in force at its end; `--unlock` with a reason removes the lock; both are audit-logged and a reason is required. The same command runs from Imports & Feeds, Periods Built (`eir-feed.lock-period`, `eir.govern`, a reason of at least ten characters; a month with no loan book cannot be locked). The lock names the month's run of record and the dialog says which ("The lock names run N"). `eir-feed.lock-period` now also takes `without_run_record`, the screen form of `--without-run-record`: it is accepted only for a month with no completed month-end or bootstrap run at all (computed before run records existed; the table shows "Open · no run record"), as an explicit tick box with a reason of at least 20 characters. A month with a month-end run on record that a screen run changed since is refused it: the month-end job is run again and the month locked normally. `App\Support\ReportingPeriodLock::assertOpen` is the shared check the writers call: staging, PD, LGD, ECL, the forward-looking route, the Mega Farm run, the revenue roll-forward, EIR reopening, write-offs (at proposal, approval and reversal), restructures and rate resets; a refused write names the month and how to unlock it. The loan book build reads `reporting_period_locks` directly and refuses a locked month, and a staging threshold change must take effect after the latest locked month.

### 6.17 Not yet implemented

Re-estimation of expected cash flows for a purchased or originated credit-impaired asset (a derecognised stage 3 loan fails closed for want of a credit-adjusted EIR); proposed adjusting journal entries (`trueup_gl_account`) and the fee reclassification journal (`fee_reclass_journal`); reproduction of the historic materiality assessment; a choice of auditor export format; a CCF model; and scheduling of the monthly run. The Governance Centre lists the settings recorded but not yet applied (`GovernanceService::notYetApplied`). `app/Console/Kernel.php` does not schedule the monthly revenue run; it is run by hand or as a step of the Maintenance month-end job (`maiic:task month-end`, chapter 9.5), whose steps include, in this order, the scheme load (`eir:load-schemes`), the restructure detection, staging, PD and LGD, the forward-looking month-end, the ledger cash (`eir:load-ledger-cash`), the GL fee build (`eir:build-gl-fees`), the revenue run and the Mega Farm programme.
