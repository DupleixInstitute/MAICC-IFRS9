## 5. IFRS 9 Engines

This chapter records what each engine computes and where the code is. Formulas are quoted from the code; where a behaviour is guarded by a lock or a closed state, the guard is named. Every engine that writes a reporting period first checks the period lock (`App\Support\ReportingPeriodLock::assertOpen()`, 5.8), and every governed value is read from the Governance Centre as at the period end with no default in code: a missing approved value stops the run with the setting named.

### 5.1 Exposure at default

The engines measure one EAD, held in `app/Services/Ecl/EclExposure.php` and used by the ECL engine, the time-phased engine, the Mega Farm programme engine, the segment ECL check and the ECL build-up:

```
gross = COALESCE(carrying_amount, 0) + COALESCE(commitments, 0) * COALESCE(facility_utilisation_rate, 1)
EAD   = 0                when the loan was written off to a nil or credit balance
        MAX(gross, 0)    otherwise
```

Undrawn commitments are converted with the facility utilisation rate as the credit conversion factor; no separate CCF model exists, and one hundred percent utilisation is assumed where the rate is null. A credit balance is owed to the customer, not an exposure, so EAD is floored at nil. A loan written off to nil (the cumulative write-off the loan book build recorded in `build_basis.ledger.written_off`, chapter 4.6) has no exposure at all, its undrawn amount included; a row with no build basis (an imported book) is measured as it stands. The loan book build also drops the undrawn amount of a facility past its maturity or written off, keeping it on the build basis. The ECL summary rows (`expected_credit_loss.total_ead`) carry the exposure before the nil floor (`EclExposure::unflooredSql()`).

The reports hub (`Ifrs9ReportsController::EAD_SQL`), the stress test (`StressTestService::EAD`) and the build-up report show exposure with the gross expression, without the floor or the write-off rule.

### 5.2 Staging and SICR

A file-imported loan book is staged on import by `app/Services/Eir/StagingClassifier.php`. It reads the arrears bucket columns of a row, takes the lower bound of the highest non-empty bucket as days past due (buckets 1 to 30, 31 to 90, 91 to 180, 181 to 270 and 271 to 360 days, with both underscore and hyphen header spellings), and applies the approved `staging_thresholds` row in force at the end of the reporting period for the facility class and tenor (`StagingThreshold::forFacility()`: a facility-class row first, else the DEFAULT row with the highest minimum tenor not above the loan's). A row with no arrears is stage 1. There is no fallback ladder: with no approved rule in force the import stops and names what is missing (until 14 October 2026 it fell back to stage 2 from 31 days and stage 3 from 181).

A loan book built from the landing zone is staged by `app/Services/Eir/StagingService.php` (`php artisan eir:stage {from} [to] [--dry-run]`), with two governed triggers:

- Days past due, counted by the build on the governed basis (`dpd_basis`, by default from the oldest overdue instalment as the RBM directive counts) and read against E-Banker's instalment chart in force at the month-end, compared with the threshold for the facility class (MEGA_FARM for a Mega Farm product group or programme GL, otherwise DEFAULT) and tenor. The seeded rows (`StagingThresholdSeeder`) are stage 2 from 31 days in every class, stage 3 from 91 days for short-term (DEFAULT from 0 months) and Mega Farm facilities and from 181 days for medium and long term (DEFAULT from 13 months); a LONG_TERM row (stage 2 from 91 days) is a future-dated proposal, inactive until signed. A balance still owed after maturity is overdue from maturity.
- Missed instalments, MAIIC's accounting-policy default trigger (`stage3_missed_instalments`, seeded "4 consecutive"): the instalments due on the chart in force at the month-end (`app/Services/Ebanker/InstalmentChartHistory.php`) against the cash receipts in the ledger to that date, net of reversals and with write-offs left out, allocated in due-date order. Only a loan with a balance owed is evaluated. A month-end with no chart in force, or whose chart demands more than the ledger has ever debited to the loan, is not evaluated and is counted on the run, never assumed.

`InstalmentChartHistory` reads the chart as it stood on a date: E-Banker regenerates a chart whenever a loan's rate or terms change, so the live rows (P2_10) are only the latest version and the earlier ones are the superseded rows (RS_02a) and the dated copies (RS_02b, RS_02c, RS_02d). Where several instalments of one monthly definition share a date (a collapsed chart), the definition's rows are dated by serial number, one month apart. The build also uses it to correct two E-Banker ageing defects, each flagged on the row: ageing reported when the chart in force had nothing due, and a collapsed chart reporting the whole balance overdue.

The DPD stage is written to `ifrs9stage_pre_qualitative`; the stage after the missed-instalment trigger, the qualitative triggers and the cure period to `ifrs9stage_post_qualitative` and `calculated_ifrs9_stage`. A trigger never moves a loan down. A move down waits for the governed cure period (`stage_cure_months`, seeded "3 months"): the loan is held at its prior stage until it has been below the threshold for that many consecutive month-ends; a move up is immediate.

Staging thresholds are under maker-checker since 14 October 2026 (`app/Services/Eir/StagingThresholdService.php`, routes `settings.staging-thresholds.propose`, `.approve` and `.reject`, on Settings, Staging basis). One person proposes a row with a reason; it takes no effect until a different person approves it. A change applies forward only: its effective date must be later than the last approved row for the same class and tenor and after the last locked reporting period. A stage 2 trigger later than 31 days rebuts the IFRS 9 B5.5.37 presumption and needs a rebuttal basis.

MAIIC's audited definition of default is four consecutive missed payments or 90 days past due, and its internal grade ladder maps 1 to 30 days to grade 3, 31 to 90 to grade 6, 91 to 180 to grade 8 and over 180 to grade 10.

Qualitative SICR is a governed register (`app/Services/Eir/QualitativeSicrService.php`), configured through `StageingRulesController`, `SicrGroupController`, `SicrItemController` and `SicrTriggerController`: alert items grouped into SICR groups, each item stating the stage it implies (2 for a significant increase in credit risk, 3 for credit-impaired evidence), and triggers raised against one contract or every loan of a customer, with evidence and the dates they are in force. A trigger is proposed by one person and approved by a second (`sicr-triggers.approve`, `.reject`, `.close`). `eir:stage` applies the approved triggers in force at the month-end, lifting the loan to the highest stage they imply, and writes `loan_books.sicr`; it no longer reads that flag.

### 5.3 Probability of default

Transition profiles (`TransitionProfileDefinitionController`, `TransitionProfileOptionController`) define which tables and grading columns feed a matrix and whether it is count or balance based. `app/Services/TransitionMatrixService.php` builds the matrix: for each grade or stage at the start of the period, the share of exposures (by count or balance) in each grade at the end. Since 14 October 2026 a loan with a nil or credit balance at the start is not an observation, and a loan the ledger wrote off inside the window counts as a default whatever stage its nil balance is given. The matrix moves through draft, calculated, locked and applied states; locking makes it immutable and applying writes the 12-month PD to `loan_books.pd_prefli`. `app/Services/TransitionMatrixCummulativeService.php` chains monthly matrices into cumulative multi-period probabilities. `app/Services/Pd/PdEngineService.php` runs the same measure for one portfolio without a request (the bootstrap and the scheduler).

The PD by segment (`app/Services/Pd/PdSegmentationService.php`, run with the LGD by `php artisan ifrs9:segment-parameters {from} [to] [--pd] [--lgd] [--window=12] [--dry-run]`) measures the PD on the governed basis (`pd_segmentation_basis`: by portfolio, seeded; pooled book; by RBM sector; or by portfolio and sector). For each segment, the loans in a stage at the start of a twelve-month window are followed to its end; the PD from a stage is the balance that reached stage 3 (or left the book unpaid, or was written off) over the balance that started in it, annualised when the book holds a shorter window. A stage is thin when it has fewer loans or defaults than `pd_segment_min_observations` (seeded "10 loans and 1 default per stage"); a thin stage takes its parent's PD under `pd_segment_thin_rule` (seeded "Take the parent: portfolio, then the pooled book"), provided the parent meets the minimum, or the run stops. Loans with an industry code held apart (`pd_sector_unverified_codes`, seeded 4290) form an "unverified sector" segment. Each segment is written to `transition_matrices` with its cells, each cell's outcome to `segment_parameter_results`, the run to `segment_parameter_runs`, and each loan carries the segment it belongs to, the segment whose PD it took, the run and the matrix. There is no other fallback: a stage with no usable PD stops the run and nothing is written.

The Mega Farm programme is kept out of the MAIIC PD (decision D30): the portfolios that hold only programme loans (every loan on GLs 1050301 to 1050307, `MegaFarmEclService::programmePortfolios()`) are left out, and a portfolio that mixes programme loans with MAIIC's own stops the run until `php artisan ifrs9:segment-portfolios` has given each programme its own portfolio. The programme's PD is its own (5.6).

Internal grades (`InternalGradingController`) are grading profiles (`internal_grade_profiles`), each with its grades (`internal_grade_mappings`: grade code, PD range, RBM class, S&P rating) and a PD per grade and tenor year (`grade_tenor_pd`). One profile is active at a time. Updating the loan book from the active profile (`internal-grading.loanbook.updateWithPD`) writes `pd_prefli` from each loan's `internal_grade_code`: stage 1 takes the one-year PD, stage 2 the PD of the whole number of years covering its remaining tenor (`App\Support\Tenor::yearsCovering()`), stage 3 one; a loan with no mapping or no PD for its tenor is left as it was and named, and a locked period is refused.

The S&P grading of MAIIC's ECL model is imported as a profile by `app/Services/Ifrs9/SpGradesImportService.php` (Internal Grades, import; `internal-grading.import-sp.preview` shows what will load, `internal-grading.import-sp` creates it). It reads the workbook's "ASS1-Internal Grades" sheet (each grade with its description, S&P mapping, broad grade and PD range) and "PDs-S&P" sheet (S&P's average cumulative default rates by rating and horizon, 1 to 10 years) and creates a draft, inactive profile: it changes no PD until someone activates it. A grade with no row of cumulative PDs (grades 7 and 9 in the December 2025 model) is reported and left out. Grade codes are unique within a profile, not across profiles, and PDs are held to eight decimal places.

The ECL by Internal Grade report groups the book by `internal_grade_code` into the A to G master scale it labels with PD bands (A up to 2 percent, B to 5, C to 10, D to 20, E to 40, F to 100, G default); it does not map a PD to a grade.

### 5.4 Loss given default

Monthly LGD (`LossGiveDefaultController`) computes, per stage, opening balances, recoveries, cures and closing balances over a period. The manual mode accepts user-entered rates. Cumulative LGD (`LossGivenDefaultCummulativeController`) aggregates across periods. Both lock once closed.

`app/Services/Lgd/LgdEngineService.php` is the cohort workout as a service. The loans in stage 3 at the start of the window with a balance owed are followed to the reporting period: a loan back in stage 1 or 2 that still owes has cured; on the loans that did not cure, the cash received is the recovery. A loan that ends the window at a nil balance is not a cure (since 14 October 2026): either its cash repaid it, a full recovery, or the rest was written off and left in the book at nil, which stages as current, and what the cash did not return is counted as written off. LGD = (1 - cure rate) x (1 - recovery rate), floored and capped, written as a system calculation to `loss_given_default` and applied to the period's loan book as `lgd_value` and `collection_lgd`. `app/Services/Lgd/LgdSegmentationService.php` measures it per portfolio over one shared window; a portfolio whose stage 3 cohort is below `lgd_segment_min_cohort` (seeded "5 Stage 3 loans at the window start") takes the pooled book's LGD under the thin-segment rule, provided the pooled book meets the minimum, or the run stops. The Mega Farm programme's LGD is its own cohort workout (5.6).

Recovery discounting: `app/Jobs/CalculateDiscountingJob.php` discounts the recovery payments of an LGD run at the rate `app/Services/Ecl/EclDiscountRateService.php` resolves on the requested basis (the effective interest rate, the loan book's contractual rate, or a manual rate). On the EIR basis a fixed-rate loan takes its locked original EIR and a floating-rate loan its current EIR (IFRS 9 B5.5.44, as the revenue engine re-estimates it); the original EIR is never used in its place. There is no default rate: a payment whose rate cannot be resolved is skipped and counted with the reason. `app/Jobs/ProcessLGDPayments.php` builds the long-format payment tracking for stage 3 contracts in chunks of 500 with a two-hour timeout.

Agricultural credit risk mitigation (off-take agreements, warehouse receipts, group guarantees, anchor buyer cover) is modelled by `php artisan ifrs9:seed-agri-risk` and reported in the Credit Risk Mitigation report.

### 5.5 Forward-looking information

The forward-looking (FLI) engine was rebuilt on 13 October 2026 to the FLI module specification (`docs/MAIIC_FLI_Module_Specification_v1_2026-10-13.md`; its section 12 lists what is built). It moves each loan's PD for the economy through one governed chain, with the model applied monthly but recalibrated only when the Governance Centre cadence asks (`fli_model_cadence`, seeded "Once a year, and when an event breaks the relationship"). The code is in `app/Services/Fli/`, with the scenario sets in `app/Services/Scenario/ScenarioSetService.php` and the as-at reader in `app/Support/Fli/AsAtSeries.php`.

```
macro statistics -> macro_series bridge -> credit loss series -> correlation -> fits under the guardrail
-> fit approved (maker, checker) publishes a model version -> scenario set, scenarios and shocks approved
-> overlays reviewed -> fli:apply writes pd_post_fli -> segment ECL on pd_post_fli, discounted
-> month-end close: ECL per scenario, weighted against booked, undiscounted and discounted -> second-person sign-off
```

Bridge: `FliBridgeService::refresh()` fills the engines' input tables from MAIIC's own and is idempotent. `macro_series` is built from `macro_statistics_data`: an annual value is held at its December and the months between two observations are interpolated and labelled as such; the vintage is the publisher's release date (`macro_statistics_data.published_at`) where recorded, otherwise the date the bridge first held the row; the prime lending rate is added monthly from `reference_rate_series`. `credit_loss_series` receives the proxies derived from the staged loan books (`NPL_RATIO`, `STAGE3_SHARE` and `DEFAULT_RATE_12M`, for the book and per product group) and the external series entered on Credit Loss Data (`macro_credit_loss_data`), each as proxy `EXT_<code>` (or `EXT_<code>_P<portfolio>`) with `known_from` set to its release month. The bridge also writes `governed_parameters` from the Governance Centre, `regression_definitions` (the expected sign of each driver) and `structural_events`. Every engine then reads the series through `AsAtSeries`, which admits only what was knowable at the end of the period: an actual after its publication lag or release date, a forecast only from the date in its vintage (an undated forecast is never knowable for a past period), and an external credit-loss row only from its `known_from` month.

Correlation and fits: `php artisan fli:correlate {period}` (also run from Forward-Looking Model, Correlation Finder, and from Maintenance) refreshes the bridge, profiles every series (`FliSeriesProfiler`, `fli_series_distributions`), sweeps every driver against every proxy over the lag grid (`CorrelationFinder`, writing `analysis_runs` and `fli_suggestions`) and fits the candidates (`RegressionEngine`, writing `fli_relationships` and `fli_fits`). `Guardrail` applies a fit only when it has enough observations (`fli_min_observations`), the expected sign (enforced or advisory under `fli_expected_sign_test`), an R-squared at or above `fli_r2_cutoff` and a p-value within `fli_alpha`; otherwise the fit is declined with the reason. Nothing here touches a PD.

Model versions: on Forward-Looking Model, Regression, one person proposes an applied fit and a different person approves it (`FliRouteService::proposeFit()` and `approveFit()`, `fli_fits.approval_status`). Approval calls `FliModelRegistry::publishFromFit()`, which writes a `fli_model_versions` row under model `PD-FLI-BOOK` (`fli_models`) carrying the fit's driver, proxy, lag, slope, intercept and diagnostics, `calibrated_period` and `effective_from` (by default the fit's own month, never earlier than its calibration month). A version is never edited: a later version published for the same month marks the earlier one SUPERSEDED, and `retire()` marks one RETIRED with a reason. A model calibrated outside the system is proposed on Scenarios & Overlays, External Calculations (`proposeExternal()`, `source` = `external`, with its `evidence`) and published only when a second person approves it on Model Versions (`approveExternal()`). The version in force for a period is `FliModelRegistry::inForce()`: the latest PUBLISHED version whose `effective_from` is on or before the period. The month-end applies that version; it does not re-fit.

Scenario sets: `ScenarioSetService` governs one set per period in `governed_scenario_sets`, versioned through DRAFT, PROPOSED, APPROVED, LOCKED and SUPERSEDED, with its scenarios in `governed_scenarios` (weight, `is_base`, narrative, calibration note) and every non-base scenario expressed as shocks on the base path in `scenario_shocks` (kind `pct`, `abs`, `replace` or `mult` per statistic and forecast year). A set is refused unless it meets the Governance Centre rules (`scenario_minimum_count`, `scenario_weight_bounds`, the calibration note on a downside) and its weights sum to 100; the rules are read with no value in code, so a missing or unreadable setting stops the check rather than falling back. A second person approves the set; the lock refuses while an overlay of the period is still proposed. A set can be started from a Scenario Profile, and `php artisan scenario:sets {period}` lists, seeds, approves and locks from the console.

Overlays: `OverlayService` keeps the register `fli_overlays`: scope (book, product group or contract), a signed fraction on the PD, reason, evidence, owner, expiry period, proposer and a different approver. Under the D-F9 double-counting check an overlay whose `risk_driver` is the driver of the model version in force for its month cannot be approved unless `double_count_review` records why the model does not capture it. An overlay is in force from its period to its `expiry_period`.

The route: `FliRouteService::apply()`, run by `php artisan fli:apply {period}`, refuses a locked reporting period and reads the route (`fli_adjustment_route`: Regression, Manual overlay or Regression plus overlay), the weighting (`scenario_weighting_method`) and the transmission method (`fli_transmission_method`, `TransmissionMethodCatalogue`) in force. For each scenario of the approved set it evaluates the version in force, proxy = slope x driver(t - lag) + intercept: the base takes the driver actual `lag` months before the period, the twelve-month window takes the driver `lag` months before its end (an actual when that is on or before the period, otherwise the scenario's path), and the adjustment is the predicted window proxy over the predicted base, less one. A missing lagged actual or path value fails closed for the period. Each loan's PD under each scenario comes from the transmission method; under the seeded weighting ("Weight the ECL across scenarios") the post-FLI PD is the twelve-month PD whose stage PD equals the probability-weighted stage PD across scenarios, so the ECL the engine books on it equals the weighted ECL; the other option weights the scenario adjustments into one. Stage 3 is set to 100 percent. Each loan receives `pd_post_fli`, `fli_adj` (post over pre, less one), `fli_route`, `fli_method`, `fli_fit_id`, `fli_set_id`, `fli_model_version_id`, `fli_by_scenario` (each scenario's weight and PD) and `fli_overlay_ids`, and the run is logged as `FLI Route Applied`. With no version in force under the regression route the PD holds and the loan records why.

Projection and impact: `FliProjectionService::run()` (Forward-Looking Model, Adjusted Forecast, or `fli:month-end --project`) projects, per portfolio and scenario, the conditional, survival, marginal and cumulative PD of each future year into `ecl_pd_term_structures` with one `ecl_projection_runs` row (`methodology_version` = `fli-projection`); inside the horizon of `fli_forecast_horizon` each year takes the scenario path, after it the adjustment reverts in equal steps to nil. It is evidence for Stage 2 lifetime losses; the booked ECL still comes from the ECL engine. `FliImpactService::simulate()` (FLI Month-End, Impact Analysis) measures any set of the month, proposed or approved, against the booked ECL without writing anything.

Month-end close: `FliMonthEnd::steps()` defines the ten steps shown on FLI Month-End, Overview and followed by `fli:month-end`: loan book staged with a PD before FLI; economic data as at the month-end; credit-loss history; model version in force; scenario set approved; overlays reviewed; route applied under the version and set in force; scenario projection (optional); ECL calculated on the post-FLI PD after the route; month recorded and signed off. The ECL step runs `ifrs9:segment-ecl {period} --pd=pd_post_fli --discounting=discounted` (5.6), so the engine measure (`ecl_value`) and the discounted allowance (`ecl_value_discounted`, with each scenario's shortfall in `ecl_by_scenario`) are both on the loans.

`FliCloseService::record()` sets the Mega Farm programme's loans aside (they are measured by their own engine and shown as their own line, `programme_loans` and `programme_ecl`; a month whose every loan is in the programme is refused) and refuses a month whose ECL is not on the post-FLI PD, was calculated before the route last ran, or mixes scenario sets. On the engine measure it measures each loan under each scenario as its booked ECL re-based from the booked stage PD to the scenario's stage PD (Stage 3 does not move), writes one `ecl_scenario_results` row per loan and scenario, and writes one `fli_run_snapshots` row with the pre-FLI ECL, model and overlay effects, booked and weighted ECL, their difference and `reconciles` (true when the difference is within the greater of one kwacha and one part in a million of the booked ECL), the totals by scenario, stage and portfolio, the inputs it ran under (model version, scenario set, overlays, PD segment runs, ECL runs, loan book loads, macro vintage) and the movement against the previous month-end. Beside it, since 14 October 2026, it records the discounted allowance (IFRS 9 5.5.17(b)): the booked discounted allowance, the same weighted over the set's scenarios from the time-phased engine's own per-scenario figures (`ecl_scenario_results.ecl_discounted`), their difference and `discounted_reconciles` (within the greater of one kwacha and half a cent per discounted loan plus one part in a million), the reconciliation from the engine measure (`projection_effect` and `discounting_effect`), and the loans that could not be discounted, named. A new record of the same month supersedes the old one and its loan rows.

`FliCloseService::signOff()` is the second person's approval: it refuses a record that is not RECORDED, a weighted ECL that does not reconcile, a discounted allowance that does not reconcile (or a loan with no per-scenario figures), a movement above `fli_movement_commentary` without a commentary, loans left undiscounted without a commentary saying why, and a sign-off by the person who recorded the month.

### 5.6 ECL calculation

`ExpectedCreditLossController::calculateECL()` runs per reporting period and scope (portfolio or sector), with the PD column (`pd_prefli` or `pd_post_fli`), the LGD (`customer_lgd`, `collection_lgd`, or `both`, their product) and the discounting mode (`undiscounted` when none is given, as in `ifrs9:recalculate-ecl`; `discounted` in the month-end). A locked period is refused. Per loan:

```
stage    = COALESCE(ifrs9stage_post_qualitative, calculated_ifrs9_stage, ifrs9stage_pre_qualitative)
months   = remaining_tenor, or 12 when it is null or below 1
stage PD = 1 - (1 - PD12) ^ (MIN(months, 12) / 12)   stage 1
           1 - (1 - PD12) ^ (months / 12)            stage 2 (lifetime)
           1                                         stage 3
ECL      = stage PD x LGD x EAD                       (EAD as in 5.1)
```

The result is written per loan to `loan_books.ecl_value` and `lgd_value` and summarised per stage into `expected_credit_loss` with the PD and LGD values used. The run fails closed before anything is written when a loan with an exposure has no stage, no PD of the chosen kind (stage 1 and 2) or no LGD; a run before FLI reads the PD before FLI only and is never filled from the PD after it. A run takes a lock per period and scope; a second run while one is in flight is refused with the message that a calculation is already running. `php artisan ifrs9:recalculate-ecl {period}` exposes the same logic with `--level`, `--portfolio`, `--sector`, `--pd`, `--lgd` and `--discounting`.

The month-end runs the ECL by segment: `php artisan ifrs9:segment-ecl {from} [to] --pd=pd_post_fli [--lgd=collection_lgd] [--discounting=discounted] [--prune-stale]` calls `calculateECL()` for every portfolio of the month except the Mega Farm programme's, after first failing closed, with the loans named, when a portfolio mixes programme loans with MAIIC's own, when a loan with an exposure has no measured stage, no PD of the chosen kind or no LGD, or when the loans carry PDs from more than one PD segment run (or from outside one). Each portfolio's old rows for the month are removed in the same transaction, and `--prune-stale` removes the rows of portfolios that no longer hold a loan. Discounting is the default since 13 October 2026. This is the ECL step of the forward-looking month-end (5.5) and of `maiic:task month-end` (chapter 9.5).

Discounted ECL (IFRS 9 5.5.17(b)): in discounted mode the time-phased engine runs on the same loans inside the same transaction. A refusal (`app/Services/Ecl/EclRunRefused.php`) rolls the whole run back and the screen or console shows the reason; nothing is estimated in its place. The single-horizon proxy that discounted a loss when no scenario set was available (`EclDiscountingService`) was removed on 14 October 2026. An undiscounted run clears what an earlier discounted run left on the loans (`ecl_discount_status` NOT_REQUESTED).

Time-phased engine: `app/Services/Ecl/TimePhasedEclService.php` projects a monthly marginal default and cash shortfall curve per scenario and discounts each month's shortfall. It leaves `ecl_value` as the ECL engine's measure, which the FLI build-up and the month record reconcile to, and writes beside it `ecl_value_discounted`, `ecl_discounting_effect` (its own undiscounted projection less the discounted), `ecl_discount_rate`, `ecl_discount_rate_source`, `ecl_discount_status` (CALCULATED_TIME_PHASED) and `ecl_by_scenario`; the month-by-month rows go to `ecl_cashflow_projections` and `ecl_pd_term_structures` and the run to `ecl_projection_runs`. Its rules:

- Scenarios: a run is on the governed set when any of its loans carries the set or a per-scenario PD from the route; then every loan is measured under that set's scenarios and weights with the per-scenario PD the route wrote, LGD and EAD unshocked, and the legacy `ecl_scenario_assumptions` are never mixed in. A defaulted loan takes the set's scenarios with no shock; a performing loan with no per-scenario PD is refused. Only when no loan carries the set are the approved legacy assumptions used (their weights must sum to one). A run before FLI is one scenario at the PD before FLI. A post-FLI run with neither is refused whole (`EclRunRefused`).
- Rate: the contract's locked original EIR; a floating-rate loan is discounted at its current EIR at the reporting date (B5.5.44) and is left unresolved and named when that cannot be determined; a negative EIR is refused. A stage 3 loan with no approved recovery plan is measured at the reporting date (EAD x LGD) and needs no rate. Any other loan without a locked EIR is named and left unresolved (`TIME_PHASED_UNRESOLVED`), never given a rate.
- Curve: the stage is the post-qualitative one; the conditional hazard is anchored to the twelve months the PD describes and run for the life of the exposure, so a five-year stage 2 lifetime compounds (a 12 percent annual PD becomes 47.2 percent over five years) and a horizon under a year carries less than the annual figure. Performing exposure amortises with the version 1 schedule. Stage 3 exposure does not amortise; where a reviewed recovery plan exists in `ecl_recovery_cashflows`, the shortfall is spread across the plan's dates.
- Exposure: EAD as in 5.1; a nil exposure is a nil loss, measured, not unresolved.
- Invariant: at a rate of nil or more the discounted loss never exceeds the undiscounted; a loan whose projection breaks this is refused (the one exception is a defaulted loan with an approved recovery plan, where discounting a recovery raises the loss).

The allowance every report carries is the booked allowance (`app/Services/Reports/BookedEcl.php`): `ecl_value_discounted` where the time-phased engine discounted the loan, the programme measure for a Mega Farm loan, and otherwise the undiscounted `ecl_value`, which the report counts and names. The ECL page and the dashboard call the figure booked only when every loan in view is discounted. The reconciliation from the engine measure to the allowance shown is the projection effect less the discounting effect, loan by loan (`EclBuildUpService::discounted()`).

Mega Farm programme (spec v4 section 16, decision D30): `app/Services/MegaFarm/MegaFarmEclService.php` (`php artisan megafarm:ecl {period}`) measures the programme's loans apart from MAIIC's book, which keeps them out of its PD, LGD, segment ECL, build-up and month record. The governed scope (`mega_farms_scope`, seeded "Out of EIR engine; in ECL module; 5 percent share on net") states MAIIC's share; a scope that states none is declined, not taken as one hundred percent. The PD comes from the governed method (`megafarm_pd_method`, seeded "Agricultural-sector PD scaled to the programme", the scalar capped at `megafarm_scalar_ceiling`); the LGD is the programme's own cohort workout, declined when there is no stage 3 cohort to follow (the typed fallbacks were removed on 13 October 2026). The programme ECL is EAD x PD x LGD; each loan carries MAIIC's share of it in `ecl_value`, and every run is recorded in `megafarm_ecl_runs` with its basis.

`tests/Feature/Ecl/EclGoldenNumberTest.php` pins the expected ECL for a fixed test population; `TimePhasedEclServiceTest.php`, `TimePhasedEclInvariantTest.php` and `TimePhasedEclFloatingRateTest.php` cover the curve by stage, recovery plans, scenario weighting, the governed set, the invariant and floating rates; `WrittenOffNilEclTest.php` the exposure rule and the refusals; `MegaFarmProgrammeSeparationTest.php` the programme separation; `EclBuildUpServiceTest.php` the build-up and the discounted reconciliation.

### 5.7 Stress testing

`Reports/StressTestingController.php` is the single stress screen (ticket #003 retired the duplicate hub tile); the loan-level engine is `app/Services/Stress/StressTestService.php`, which the bootstrap also runs for every scenario of the approved set. It works on the ECL basis, by the stage the ECL was measured on (post-qualitative first) and the same stage PD rule as 5.6:

```
base   = ecl_value
stress = base x (stage PD(MIN(1, PD12 x multiplier[stage])) / stage PD(PD12)) x (MIN(1, LGD + addon[stage]) / LGD)
```

so the base case (multipliers 1, add-ons 0) equals the ECL engine's measure to the kwacha. A loan with no ECL, or a nil PD or LGD, is measured directly as EAD x stage PD x LGD. PD12 is `pd_post_fli`, else `pd_prefli`. PD multipliers default to 1 and are floored at 0.01; LGD add-ons are entered in percentage points and floored at zero. Results roll up by stage and by portfolio with base, stressed, delta and delta percent. Macro mode derives one uniform multiplier from a saved regression model or a manual slope and intercept: predicted base equals slope times base macro plus intercept, shocked macro equals base times one plus the shock percent, and the multiplier is one plus the ratio change, floored at 0.01. Scenarios can be saved with a result snapshot (`stress_scenarios`). `tests/Feature/StressTestingReconciliationTest.php` checks the driver arithmetic, the caps under extreme shocks, the macro derivation and the retired hub redirect.

### 5.8 Locks, closed states and sign-off

| Object | Guard |
|---|---|
| Reporting period | Locked or unlocked with a reason (`reporting_period_locks`; Imports & Feeds, Periods Built, or `eir:lock-period`). A locked month is never restated: the loan book build, staging, the PD and LGD engines and their segment runs, the internal-grade PD, the forward-looking route, the ECL, the Mega Farm run, the collateral allocation and customer LGD, the revenue roll-forward, reopening an EIR, a write-off proposal, restructure and rate-reset confirmations and a staging threshold change all refuse it |
| Transition matrix, cumulative matrix | Locked matrices cannot be edited; create a new draft instead |
| LGD monthly and cumulative | Closed runs are immutable |
| ECL run | One run per period and scope at a time; a discounted run that is refused is rolled back whole |
| Staging threshold | Proposed by one person, applied only once a different person approves it, effective forward only and after the last locked period |
| Qualitative SICR trigger | Proposed by one person, applied only once a second person approves it; closing sets its end date, so the months it covered keep it |
| Loan book build, take-on build | Requested by one person and approved by another (or under the bootstrap's automated label, never read as a MAIIC approval) |
| Financial period | Closing a period prevents postings into it |
| EIR original rate | Locked after maker and checker approval; reopening is administrator-only with a stated reason (chapter 6) |
| FLI model version | Never edited once published; replaced from the same month as SUPERSEDED, or withdrawn as RETIRED with a reason, and the months it governed keep pointing at it |
| Scenario set | Approved by a second person; a LOCKED set is final and a change is a new version with a reason |
| FLI route | Refused for a locked reporting period |
| FLI month record | Signed off by a second person only when the weighted ECL reconciles to the booked ECL, the weighted discounted allowance reconciles to the one booked, and any movement above the tolerance, or any loan left undiscounted, carries a commentary |
